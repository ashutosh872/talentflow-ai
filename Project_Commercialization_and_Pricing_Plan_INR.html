1. Free vs Paid Cloud Services & Builder Infrastructure (INR ₹)

As a solo developer in Delhi, your primary goal is to minimize fixed operational burn. You do not need expensive cloud contracts. Below is an exact audit of what you can run on 100% Free Tiers versus when and why you should upgrade to paid tools:

Component 100% Free Tier (MVP / Launch) Paid Tier (Scale Phase) Founder Recommendation
Database & Storage Supabase Free: 500 MB PostgreSQL, 50k MAU, built-in Row-Level Security (₹0). Supabase Pro: ₹2,100 / mo ($25). 8 GB storage, daily automated backups, no pausing. Start FREE. 500 MB easily stores 50,000 candidate applications and replies.
Web Hosting & Server Vercel / Render Free: Global CDN, automatic HTTPS, 100 GB bandwidth / mo (₹0). Hetzner Cloud VPS: ₹450 / mo (€4.5) for 4GB RAM 2 vCPU Linux server in Germany/Helsinki. Start FREE. Move long-running scraper background workers to a cheap ₹450/mo VPS later.
LLM AI Engine Google Gemini 1.5 Flash: 15 Requests/min, 1,500 Requests/day 100% FREE! Gemini Pay-As-You-Go: ₹0.006 per 1k input tokens. ~₹350/mo for 500k tokens. Start FREE. 1,500 daily requests easily handles 50 active daily users at zero cost!
Job Scraping & Feeds Direct ATS APIs: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby public JSON boards (₹0). Adzuna 250 calls/day free. JSearch RapidAPI: ₹2,100 / mo ($25) for 10,000 real-time global search calls. Hybrid. Use direct ATS feeds for 80% of jobs; supplement with JSearch API for niche roles.
Inbound Gmail Poller Node.js IMAP (Port 993): Direct TLS socket to Gmail IMAP. 100% free forever (₹0). Third-party APIs (Nylas / Aurinko): ₹8,000+ / mo ($99/mo). Keep your IMAP engine! It is already working and costs you ₹0.
Payment Gateway Razorpay Standard: ₹0 setup fee. 2% + 18% GST flat per successful domestic transaction. Cashfree / Stripe India: Similar 2% transaction fee structure. Razorpay UPI Autopay. Instant QR codes and recurring e-mandates on PhonePe & GPay.
Domain & Tunnel Cloudflare Free + .in Domain: ₹499 / year for domain. Cloudflare SSL & Quick Tunnels are ₹0. Cloudflare Pro: ₹1,700 / mo ($20). Advanced Web Application Firewall rules. Buy a ₹499/year .in domain (e.g. TalentFlow.in). Use Cloudflare's free tier.
Total Fixed Operational Cost to Launch: ₹499 for the entire year (for your .in domain name). Your database, hosting, AI model, Gmail poller, and payment gateway are completely covered under free tiers for your first 250 registered users!
