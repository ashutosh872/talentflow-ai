🧠 2. High-Yield Technical Interview Questions & Answers
Q1 In high-velocity product teams like Zomato/Blinkit, how do you rapidly deliver pixel-perfect UI from Figma without cutting corners on code quality?

I utilize a reusable component mindset. Before writing a line of code for a new screen, I break the Figma design into atomic components (buttons, badges, item cards, price pills). By leveraging existing design tokens for typography, spacing, and colors, 70% of the screen is assembled from pre-tested primitives, allowing me to focus on custom micro-interactions and edge-case responsive behaviors.
